Biography

Lung-Hao Lee is an Associate Professor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence Innovation, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. His research interests include natural language processing, medical language understanding, dimensional sentiment analysis, information retrieval, and information extraction.

Before joining NYCU, he served as a faculty member in the Department of Electrical Engineering at National Central University, and as a postdoctoral fellow at National Taiwan University and National Taiwan Normal University.
