Research

Research

Research at the intersection of natural language processing, large language models, healthcare, sentiment and emotion, and information retrieval.

Language Intelligence

Natural Language Processing & Large Language Models

I study methods for language understanding and generation, including representation learning, multilingual modeling, structured prediction, and adaptation of large language models to domain-specific tasks.

LLMsMultilingual NLPInformation ExtractionStructured Prediction

Human-Centered AI

Medical Language Understanding

My medical NLP research explores how language technologies can support healthcare information access and understanding, including healthcare named entity recognition, medical question answering, mental-health text analysis, and clinical decision support.

Healthcare NLPMedical QAMental Health NLPClinical Text

Affective Computing

Dimensional Sentiment & Emotion Analysis

I investigate fine-grained affective representations beyond categorical polarity, with emphasis on valence–arousal modeling, dimensional sentiment resources, aspect-based sentiment analysis, and multilingual emotion understanding.

Valence–ArousalDimensional SentimentABSAEmotion

Search & Retrieval

Information Retrieval & Semantic Matching

I work on semantic retrieval and ranking methods for finding relevant information from large collections, including dense retrieval, neural reranking, semantic similarity, and retrieval-augmented language applications.

Dense RetrievalNeural RerankingSemantic SimilarityRAG
